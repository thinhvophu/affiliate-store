# Feature Specification: Traffic Foundations
**ID:** `F0016`
**Epic/Module:** SEO & Content Pipeline
**Status:** Draft

---

## 1. Business Objective
After ~6 weeks live at `https://www.muagear.com`, the site has had **4 real organic sessions** (GA4 property 413699722; analysis in `docs/ideas/1-traffic-improvement-plan.md`). The traffic analysis found five problems that are fixable inside this repository and are holding back indexing and ranking:

1. Every canonical URL points to a redirect. Canonical, `og:url`, JSON-LD, breadcrumbs, `sitemap.xml` and internal links all use the trailing-slash form (`/bai-viet/x/`), and the live server answers it with `308 → /bai-viet/x`. Verified with `curl -I` on 2026-10-18.
2. Weekly deal roundups each create a new dated URL for the same query. Ten of the 14 posts are `deal-<category>-<date>`, which spreads ranking signals across near-duplicate pages (keyword cannibalization).
3. Deal headings are raw Shopee seller titles (`… | Quang Học | 5 Nút Lập Trình | …`), and the same products repeat week after week. The pages read as an auto-generated scraped mirror.
4. No real author. Article JSON-LD names the `MuaGear` Organization as author, which Google's reviews system weighs poorly for affiliate content.
5. Thin internal linking. Category pages, guides, deal pages and product pages barely link to each other.

This feature fixes all five so that the URLs Google is given resolve with `200`, the authority of each category's deals builds up on one URL, and every page looks editorial, attributable and well connected.

## 2. Scope
### In Scope
* One canonical URL style with **no trailing slash** (root `/` excepted) everywhere a URL is composed: canonical, `og:url`, JSON-LD, breadcrumbs, `sitemap.xml`, internal `<Link>`s, pagination base paths, MDX body links, and the deal-roundup templates
* `verify:canonical` extended to fail on any sampled canonical or sitemap URL that answers 3xx
* One fixed roundup URL per category (`/bai-viet/deal-<category>`), refreshed in place by `/deal-roundup`, with a new optional `updatedAt` frontmatter field surfaced as a visible "Cập nhật ngày …", Article `dateModified` and sitemap `lastModified`
* Retirement of F0015's supersession mechanism (`scripts/deal-roundup/supersede.ts`, the archive branch of `lib/roundups.ts`), since only one roundup per category exists
* Migration of the 10 existing dated roundups: permanent redirects to their category's fixed URL, then deletion of the dated posts, their sidecars and any staged images nothing references any more
* Deal display-name cleanup (`cleanDealName()`), with the boilerplate rules shared between the product guard and a new deal guard
* Skipping products featured in recent roundups, backed by a committed per-category history of featured `itemId`+`shopId`
* An author registry (`lib/authors.ts`), a `/tac-gia/[slug]` author page, a linked byline, a `Person` Article author, and a "Cách chúng tôi chọn sản phẩm" methodology note on every post
* Internal links: category → guides and deal page; deal page → guides and category page; guide → its category's deal page; product → the posts that embed it; related posts prefer guides over deal pages

### Out of Scope
* Google Search Console verification, sitemap resubmission and indexing requests (operator work, plan item #2; F0014/US00145 owns the GSC setup)
* Writing new evergreen buying guides or doing keyword research (editorial, plan item #3a)
* Growing the catalog or adding categories (plan item #4; goes through F0012)
* Distribution, social posting and backlinks (plan item #6)
* GA4 internal-traffic and bot filters, and the GA4 ↔ GSC link (GA admin console, plan item #9)
* Hands-on review notes and own product photography (editorial work; this feature only provides the author/methodology surfaces they will live in)
* `noindex`-ing any page
* Re-tuning the scrape tool's ranking, or re-sorting/scoring deals in this repo
* Cron automation of `/deal-roundup` (still operator-triggered, per F0015)
* Google AdSense

## 3. Process Flow
1. **One-time URL fix (US00161):** a developer switches `buildCanonicalPath()` and every hardcoded internal path to the no-slash form and deploys. The operator runs `npm run verify:canonical -- --base=https://www.muagear.com`, which now fails if any sampled URL redirects, and resubmits the sitemap in GSC (outside this feature).
2. **One-time consolidation (US00162 → US00163):** the roundup pipeline is switched to fixed URLs, then the 10 dated posts are redirected to `/bai-viet/deal-<category>` and deleted.
3. **Weekly operator run (US00162, US00164, US00165):**
   1. The operator runs `/deal-roundup <category>`.
   2. The command scrapes, and `loadRankedDeals()` skips products featured in the last N runs, backfilling in the tool's own order.
   3. The generator cleans each deal name and overwrites `content/posts/deal-<category>.mdx` and its sidecar, keeping `publishedAt` and setting `updatedAt` to today.
   4. The featured-product history is appended.
   5. The prose step writes the Vietnamese copy (the operator may refine names).
   6. `npm test` guards the names, and the push to `main` deploys.
4. **Reader journey (US00166, US00167):**
   1. A visitor lands on a category page and sees its buying guides and its deal page.
   2. In a guide, they see a named author linking to `/tac-gia/<slug>`, a methodology note, and a "Xem deal tuần này" link.
   3. On a product page, they see the posts that mention that product.

## 4. User Stories & Acceptance Criteria

### US00161: Canonical URLs resolve with 200, not a redirect
**As a** site owner,
**I want to** have every canonical, `og:url`, JSON-LD URL, breadcrumb, sitemap `<loc>` and internal link use the trailing-slash-free URL form,
**So that** Google indexes the exact URL it is given instead of reporting "Page with redirect" or a conflicting canonical.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Scenario 1:** Composed URLs use the canonical form
    * `Given` any route (home, listings, category, product, post, policy, About)
    * `When` the site is built
    * `Then` its `<link rel="canonical">`, `og:url`, JSON-LD `url`/`@id`/`mainEntityOfPage`, breadcrumb `item` URLs and its `sitemap.xml` `<loc>` all have no trailing slash (only the root is `/`), and `buildCanonicalPath()` in `lib/seo.ts` remains the only function that composes them

* **Scenario 2:** Internal links use the canonical form
    * `Given` any `<Link>`, pagination `BASE_PATH`, breadcrumb `href`, MDX body link in `content/posts/`, or link emitted by the deal-roundup templates
    * `When` it is rendered
    * `Then` it points at the no-slash URL, and a Vitest guard fails naming the file and line if any internal `href` ending in `/` (other than `/`) appears in `app/`, `components/`, `lib/`, `content/posts/` or `scripts/deal-roundup/`

* **Scenario 3:** The deployment verifier catches redirecting canonicals
    * `Given` `npm run verify:canonical -- --base=<url>` against a deployment
    * `When` a sampled page's canonical URL, or any sampled `sitemap.xml` `<loc>`, answers with a 3xx status
    * `Then` the command exits non-zero naming each redirecting URL and its `location`, and a deployment where every checked URL answers `200` passes

* **Scenario 4:** Legacy trailing-slash links keep working
    * `Given` an external link or bookmark to the old `/bai-viet/<slug>/` form
    * `When` it is requested
    * `Then` it permanently redirects (308) to the no-slash canonical URL (Next.js default behaviour, kept, not reimplemented)

### US00162: One evergreen deal URL per category
**As a** site owner,
**I want to** have each category's weekly roundup live at one fixed URL, `/bai-viet/deal-<category>`, refreshed in place on every run,
**So that** ranking signals build up on a single page instead of being split across near-duplicate dated posts.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Scenario 1:** A run refreshes the fixed post
    * `Given` `/deal-roundup chuot-gaming` with usable deals
    * `When` the generator runs
    * `Then` it writes `content/posts/deal-chuot-gaming.mdx` and `content/deals/deal-chuot-gaming.json` (overwriting any existing ones), deal ids follow `deal-chuot-gaming-<n>`, and no dated slug is created

* **Scenario 2:** Freshness is signalled without changing the publish date
    * `Given` an existing fixed roundup with `publishedAt: "2026-10-12"`
    * `When` it is refreshed on 2026-10-19
    * `Then` `publishedAt` stays `"2026-10-12"`, new optional frontmatter `updatedAt: "2026-10-19"` is set, the post header shows "Cập nhật ngày 19 tháng 10, 2026" via `lib/format.ts`, Article JSON-LD carries `dateModified: "2026-10-19"`, and the post's sitemap entry has `lastModified` 2026-10-19

* **Scenario 3:** First run for a category
    * `Given` no `deal-<category>` post exists yet
    * `When` the generator runs
    * `Then` `publishedAt` and `updatedAt` are both today

* **Scenario 4:** Supersession is retired
    * `Given` the fixed-URL model, where each category has at most one roundup
    * `When` the feature ships
    * `Then` `scripts/deal-roundup/supersede.ts` and its call in `generate-deal-post.ts` are removed, `lib/roundups.ts`'s archive/demotion logic is removed or reduced to the one-roundup-per-category invariant, and the homepage and related-posts call sites no longer filter roundups

* **Scenario 5:** Zero usable deals leaves last week live
    * `Given` a run where no deal qualifies
    * `When` the generator runs
    * `Then` nothing is written, the existing post (with its older "Cập nhật" date) stays live, and the command exits 0 reporting the shortfall

* **Scenario 6:** Posts without `updatedAt` are unaffected
    * `Given` an evergreen guide with no `updatedAt`
    * `When` it renders
    * `Then` no "Cập nhật" line is shown, `dateModified` falls back to `publishedAt`, and sitemap `lastModified` is unchanged from today's behaviour

### US00163: Consolidate the dated roundups into the evergreen URLs
**As a** site owner,
**I want to** permanently redirect each existing dated roundup (`deal-<category>-<date>`) to its category's fixed URL and then remove it,
**So that** whatever link equity and indexing the dated posts have is consolidated onto one page per category.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Scenario 1:** Dated URLs redirect to the evergreen URL
    * `Given` `/bai-viet/deal-chuot-gaming-2026-10-05`, with or without a trailing slash
    * `When` it is requested
    * `Then` it answers a permanent redirect to `/bai-viet/deal-chuot-gaming`, and this holds for all 10 dated slugs currently in `content/posts/`

* **Scenario 2:** The newest dated roundup seeds the evergreen post
    * `Given` the three categories' newest dated roundups (2026-10-12)
    * `When` the migration runs
    * `Then` each becomes `deal-<category>.mdx` + sidecar with `publishedAt` set to its category's **earliest** dated roundup date and `updatedAt` set to its own date, so no category loses its current content before the next weekly run

* **Scenario 3:** Dated content is removed
    * `Given` the migration is complete
    * `When` the site is built
    * `Then` no dated roundup MDX file, sidecar, or staged image that nothing references any more remains, and none of the dated slugs appear in `/bai-viet` or `sitemap.xml`

* **Scenario 4:** Redirects cannot shadow a live post
    * `Given` a redirect source whose slug matches an existing post in `content/posts/`
    * `When` the site is built or `npm test` runs
    * `Then` it fails naming the colliding slug

### US00164: Clean deal display names
**As a** reader,
**I want to** see deal headings such as "Razer DeathAdder Essential" instead of seller titles stuffed with `|` segments and promo keywords,
**So that** a roundup reads as editorial content rather than a scraped mirror.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Scenario 1:** The generator cleans names
    * `Given` the raw title `"Chuột Gaming Razer DeathAdder Essential | Quang Học | 5 Nút Lập Trình | Led Đơn | Đệm Cao Cu"`
    * `When` the generator runs
    * `Then` a pure `cleanDealName()` in `scripts/deal-roundup/` returns a name with no `|`-separated tail segments, no `[…]`/`【…】` promo segments, no `!`, and none of "chính hãng", "giá rẻ", `BH n tháng`, "Freeship", "CAM KẾT" (case-insensitive), at most 70 characters cut at a word boundary. That name is written to both the sidecar `name` and the post's per-deal `h3`

* **Scenario 2:** One rule set for products and deals
    * `Given` the seller-boilerplate patterns that `lib/products.test.ts` asserts today
    * `When` this story ships
    * `Then` the patterns live in one shared module imported by the product guard, the new deal guard and `cleanDealName()`, so the rule sets cannot drift

* **Scenario 3:** A dirty name fails the guard
    * `Given` a sidecar deal whose `name` breaks a rule (for example a hand edit reintroducing `|`)
    * `When` `npm test` runs
    * `Then` it fails naming the deal id and the rule broken

* **Scenario 4:** Cleaning never empties a name
    * `Given` a raw title that would be empty or under 3 characters after cleaning
    * `When` the generator runs
    * `Then` the deal is dropped and reported in the run summary with the reason, never written with an empty name

* **Scenario 5:** The operator may refine names
    * `Given` the `/deal-roundup` prose step
    * `When` the operator hand-edits a cleaned name in the sidecar and `h3`
    * `Then` the guard still applies, and the command's instructions say to keep the sidecar `name` and `h3` in sync

### US00165: Skip recently featured products
**As a** returning reader,
**I want to** see products in this week's roundup that weren't featured in the last few weeks,
**So that** the page is worth revisiting and doesn't look auto-generated.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Scenario 1:** Recently featured deals are skipped and backfilled in tool order
    * `Given` a committed per-category history of featured products keyed by `itemId`+`shopId` (persisted from the raw snapshot), and a default `--repeat-window=3`
    * `When` `loadRankedDeals()` takes the top N
    * `Then` it skips any deal featured in the last 3 runs for that category and backfills with the next deals in the tool's own order. It never re-sorts or re-scores (F0015 §2 still holds for ordering)

* **Scenario 2:** The history is recorded
    * `Given` a successful run that writes a roundup
    * `When` the generator finishes
    * `Then` the run's date and each featured deal's `itemId`+`shopId` are appended to that category's history, and the sidecar persists `itemId`+`shopId` per deal

* **Scenario 3:** A shortfall is not an error
    * `Given` fewer than N deals remain after skipping
    * `When` the run completes
    * `Then` it writes what is available, the summary reports "skipped as recent: K" alongside the existing "requested N, usable M" footer, and the exit code is 0

* **Scenario 4:** Same-day re-runs don't double-count
    * `Given` a category already run today
    * `When` `/deal-roundup` is re-run the same day
    * `Then` today's history entry is replaced, not appended, and today's own picks are not treated as "recent" against themselves

* **Scenario 5:** The window is configurable
    * `Given` `--repeat-window=0`
    * `When` the run executes
    * `Then` no deal is skipped for recency, and a negative or non-integer value is rejected by the argument parser

### US00166: A real author and a visible methodology
**As a** site owner,
**I want to** attribute posts to a real person with a bio page, and show a short "how we pick" note on every post,
**So that** readers and Google's reviews system see accountable, first-party content rather than anonymous affiliate pages.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Scenario 1:** One author registry
    * `Given` `lib/authors.ts` registering each author's slug, display name, bio (Vietnamese) and optional avatar
    * `When` a post's frontmatter has no `author`
    * `Then` it resolves to the registry's primary author, and an `author` value not in the registry fails `next build` naming the post slug and the unknown author

* **Scenario 2:** The byline links to an author page
    * `Given` any post
    * `When` it renders
    * `Then` the byline shows the author's display name linking to `/tac-gia/<slug>`. That page is statically generated per registered author with a single `h1`, the bio, a methodology section, the author's posts (newest first), canonical/OG metadata via `buildPageMetadata()`, `Person` JSON-LD via `<JsonLd>`, and a visible breadcrumb with matching `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD

* **Scenario 3:** Article JSON-LD names a person
    * `Given` any post
    * `When` its Article JSON-LD is built
    * `Then` `author` is `{ "@type": "Person", name, url }` (the author page's absolute URL), and `publisher` remains the `MuaGear` `Organization`

* **Scenario 4:** Every post shows a methodology note
    * `Given` an evergreen guide
    * `When` it renders
    * `Then` a short "Cách chúng tôi chọn sản phẩm" note appears and links to the author page's methodology section

* **Scenario 5:** Deal posts get honest wording
    * `Given` a deal roundup
    * `When` it renders
    * `Then` the note says the picks come from Shopee's discount/rating/sales ranking and were not tested hands-on

* **Scenario 6:** No fake author ships
    * `Given` the registry still holds a placeholder (no real name or bio provided yet)
    * `When` `npm test` runs
    * `Then` it fails naming the placeholder field, so a fabricated person can never be published

* **Scenario 7:** The author page is in the sitemap
    * `Given` the author pages
    * `When` `sitemap.xml` is built
    * `Then` each `/tac-gia/<slug>` is listed

### US00167: Internal links between categories, guides, deals and products
**As a** visitor (and a crawler),
**I want to** move between a category's page, its buying guides, its deal page and the products they mention,
**So that** every page is reachable within a few clicks and authority flows to related pages.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
* **Scenario 1:** Category page → guides and deal page
    * `Given` `/danh-muc/chuot-gaming` with evergreen guides and a deal page in that category
    * `When` it renders
    * `Then` a "Hướng dẫn chọn mua" section lists that category's evergreen guides (non-roundup posts, newest first) and links to `/bai-viet/deal-chuot-gaming`. The section is omitted entirely when the category has neither

* **Scenario 2:** Deal page → guides and category page
    * `Given` a deal page
    * `When` it renders
    * `Then` it links to the same category's evergreen guides and to its category page

* **Scenario 3:** Guide → deal page
    * `Given` an evergreen guide in a category that has a deal page
    * `When` it renders
    * `Then` it shows a "Xem deal tuần này" link to that deal page, and its related-posts section ranks evergreen guides ahead of deal pages

* **Scenario 4:** Product page → posts that mention it
    * `Given` `/san-pham/<slug>` for a product embedded via `<ProductCard slug>` in one or more posts
    * `When` it renders
    * `Then` a "Bài viết nhắc đến sản phẩm này" list links to those posts. It is omitted when no post embeds the product

* **Scenario 5:** The embed index is derived in one place
    * `Given` a post that embeds the same product twice
    * `When` the product-to-posts index is built
    * `Then` the post is listed once, and the index is derived in one `lib/` module (no ad-hoc MDX parsing in pages)

## 5. Edge Cases & Error Handling
* **External links to the old trailing-slash URLs** keep working through Next.js's default 308 (US00161 S4). The fix must not add `trailingSlash: true`, which would invert the problem.
* **MDX bodies with hardcoded `/…/` links** (for example the dated roundups' cross-links) are caught by the US00161 guard. Most of them disappear with US00163's deletions anyway.
* **Redirects must be config-level** (`next.config.ts` `redirects()`). Pages stay SSG-only; no middleware and no request-time route handler.
* **Redirect source collides with a new post slug:** fail loudly (US00163 S4), never silently shadow a live post.
* **Staged deal images still referenced:** US00163 must not delete an image referenced by any remaining post or sidecar. Deletion is driven by "unreferenced", not by filename pattern.
* **Zero usable deals after recency-skipping:** treated exactly like F0015's zero-deal case. Nothing is written and last week's post stays live.
* **Snapshot missing `itemId`/`shopId`** for a deal: that deal cannot be tracked for recency. It is dropped and reported, not featured untracked.
* **The history file is missing or malformed:** a missing file means no history (first run); a malformed file is fatal and names the path.
* **`cleanDealName()` removes everything:** drop and report (US00164 S4).
* **Author registry placeholder:** blocks `npm test` (US00166 S6). Shipping the author surfaces requires the real name, bio and optional photo from the site owner.
* **A category with no guides and no deal page:** the US00167 sections render nothing, never an empty heading.
* **`updatedAt` earlier than `publishedAt`, or malformed:** fails the build naming the post slug.

## 6. Technical Considerations
* **Decisions recorded at approval (2026-10-18):**
  * **D1** URL style is no trailing slash, matching the live server's behaviour, the header nav and the landing-page URLs GA4 has already recorded.
  * **D2** Dated roundups are redirected and deleted, not kept as archives.
  * **D3** The default recency window is 3 runs.
  * **D4** Real author details are supplied by the site owner. Until then, the placeholder guard blocks publishing.
* **Chokepoints stay chokepoints:**
  * canonical composition stays in `lib/seo.ts` (`buildCanonicalPath`);
  * dates stay in `lib/format.ts` ("Cập nhật ngày …" uses `formatPostDate`);
  * breadcrumbs stay in `lib/breadcrumbs.ts`;
  * JSON-LD is emitted through `<JsonLd>` only;
  * sidecar reads stay in `lib/deals.ts`;
  * affiliate URLs are validated in `lib/affiliate.ts` only.
  New single-source modules: `lib/authors.ts` (author registry), a shared seller-boilerplate rule module (US00164), and a product→posts embed index in `lib/` (US00167).
* **F0015 amendments:**
  * US00162 retires supersession (`supersede.ts`, `formatArchiveDate()` if unused afterwards, the archive logic in `lib/roundups.ts`).
  * US00165 relaxes "`loadRankedDeals()` never filters" to "never sorts or scores; may skip recently featured items". CLAUDE.md's F0015 conventions, `scripts/README.md` and `.claude/commands/deal-roundup.md` must be updated in the same stories.
* **US00163 is destructive.** It deletes 10 posts, their sidecars and unreferenced staged images. The deletion list must be shown in the PR for review before merge.
* **SSG only:** author pages use `generateStaticParams`, redirects live in `next.config.ts`, and no route opts out of static generation.
* **Existing guards must keep passing:** `lib/posts.test.ts` publish-grade depth (≥800 words, ≥2 `h2`, ≥1 embed, 50–160-char summary), `lib/products.test.ts` catalog quality, and the image-dimension guard. The fixed deal post must clear the same depth floor every week.
* **Post-deploy operator steps (not stories):** run `npm run verify:canonical -- --base=https://www.muagear.com`, resubmit `sitemap.xml` in GSC, and request indexing for the evergreen guides, category pages and the three `deal-<category>` URLs.
* **Story order:** US00161 → US00162 → US00163; US00164, US00165, US00166 and US00167 are independent of each other but should follow US00161 (link form) and, for US00167, US00162 (fixed deal URLs).
